# Direction routes — detail for step 2

`SKILL.md` names the eight routes and their precedence. This file says what each one asks of the
build. Read the row for the route you named; nothing else here is needed.

## Precedence in practice

When more than one route fits, the highest-precedence source governs and the others work inside
it. A system plus references is system-led, with the references bounded to what the system leaves
open. A selected study contract plus a system is approved-direction, encoded through the system.
A starter is never promoted above anything a person supplied. A starter the person selected is
approved-direction; its composition still names library ids, so its build may open the files those
ids name, and nothing else in the library.

## approved-direction

The accepted contract (and its accepted amendments) or the selected starter is the specification.
Resemblance to it is expected, not a defect. Distance is **suspended**: you follow it, you do not
rotate away from it. Record the contract or starter in the stamp's `direction`.

## system-led

The approved system and the shipped surfaces own the direction. Read the shipped surfaces before
building: their component vocabulary is the vocabulary. "Make it feel fresh" is not a request for a
new direction — find freshness inside the range the system licenses (an unspent display size, an
under-used composition). A new direction is a person's decision: say so and ask. Under a locked
system the distance test is **inverted** (`variation.md`).

## reference-led and reference-directed

Invoke `/direction-study` in single-contract mode and freeze what it returns. A reference-led
builder receives the contract, real assets and the assigned original references themselves — not
only source IDs, summaries or finding cards. Keep unrequested starters, rejected concepts and
unassigned reference material out. The contract records the premise, source responsibilities,
traits that must not transfer, decisive choices, proof scope, motion intent and post-proposal
provenance. Expressive treatments the contract evidences are kept; generic
anti-pattern guidance does not flatten them, and accessibility still binds. Reference-directed
applies only on the person's explicit request and records what may be followed closely.

The `/design-review` similarity lens compares the build with the original references; tell it where
they are. Distance is **suspended**.

## brief-originated

A rich brief, no visual direction. Develop original directions through `/direction-study` from the
audience, content and desired experience. Do not open the starter catalogue merely because visual
direction is missing. Offer `/starter-directions` only when the person says they have no idea,
explicitly asks for ready-made directions, or hands off the choice after original exploration.

## starter-led

A sparse brief, no references, no system, and the direction handed to you. **Sparse** means the
brief cannot answer the one question (`discovery.md`): who it is for, what they come to do, and how
it should feel. A brief that answers it is **rich**: use brief-originated. Invoke
`/starter-directions`: it asks what it must, recommends two or three, and waits. Commit to
**exactly one** of: a starter the person selected (then the route becomes approved-direction), or a
custom direction built from the brief (`custom-direction.md`, on demand). Going custom forfeits the
starter's signature moves and ambition — say what you are giving up. If the person then supplies
references, the route becomes reference-led.

**Nobody to ask.** If the person handed the choice back or cannot be reached, pick one of the
recommended starters (or custom), say which and why, and keep the route **starter-led** with
distance **applied**: only a person's selection makes a starter the approved direction.

**A starter's composition** names structures and parts from the library by id. Open those files only
(the library index in `SKILL.md` § On demand says where they live), and if a named structure's
*Avoid when* fits
this brief, pick another the starter's idea allows and say so — the structure file wins over the
starter's suggestion.

## named-starter match

Runs only when a starter has been named — by the person, or because they asked whether one fits.
Never search the starters for a match on a supplied direction: that search is the anchoring this
route exists to prevent. `/starter-directions` runs the match gate on that one starter (four of
five dimensions) and states the boundary. Fails the gate → reference-led or system-led.
Distance is **suspended**.

## product-interface

App shells, dashboards, settings, data-dense tools. The system and the existing product surfaces
direct it; page structures and starters do not apply (a settings screen has no hero). Supplied
brand and references still bind. The production bar (`passes.md`) is the same; spend the extra
care on states, density, keyboard paths and the cost of a wrong click.
