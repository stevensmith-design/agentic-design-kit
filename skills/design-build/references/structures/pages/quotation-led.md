# `quotation-led` — Quotation led

## Shape

A real quotation from a real person leads the page and frames what follows.

## Reach for it when

The brief supplies a quote, attributed, that says the thing better than the brand could.

## Avoid when

There is no real quote (never invent one); the quote is generic praise.

## Treatments

- **Section heads:** section heads quieter than the quote.
- **Body:** body continues the quote's thought.
- **Dividers:** dividers are space.
- **Buttons:** the CTA follows the evidence, not the quote.
- **Images:** a portrait only if supplied.
- **Reveal:** no typewriter reveal.

## Variation settings

- **quote-scale:** display · large-text
- **attribution:** under · beside-with-portrait

Change at least one when this structure is used again (`check-distance.mjs`).

## Mobile

The quote steps down one rung; attribution stays attached.
