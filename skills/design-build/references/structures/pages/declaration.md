# `declaration` — Declaration

## Shape

A stance in a handful of short, numbered statements, each given room; the product appears as the
consequence of the stance.

## Reach for it when

The brand has a real point of view the visitor can agree or disagree with.

## Avoid when

The statements are platitudes anyone would sign; the product needs explaining; there is no voice.

## Treatments

- **Section heads:** statements are the headings, large, numbered.
- **Body:** body is one sentence of support per statement, or none.
- **Dividers:** dividers are full-width rules or flat colour changes.
- **Buttons:** one CTA at the end, phrased as joining or agreeing.
- **Images:** no media.
- **Reveal:** statements present at load.

## Variation settings

- **statement-count:** 3 · 5 · 7
- **statement-label:** none · key-word · count-only-when-it-is-the-claim

Change at least one when this structure is used again (`check-distance.mjs`).

## Mobile

Statements keep their numbers in the flow; size steps down one rung.
