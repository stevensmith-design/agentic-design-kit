# Proof

Evidence that others use and value the product. Every quote, name, logo and number is real and
supplied; otherwise a labelled placeholder or nothing.

Each part lists its variation settings. A part used again changes at least one
(`check-distance.mjs`); under a locked system nav and footer are shared instead.

### `marginal-quote`

A short quote set in the margin beside the text it supports.

Settings: side (left | right) · attribution (name | name-and-role)

Mobile: Moves inline after its paragraph.

### `hairline-logo-row`

Supplied customer logos in one row, one height, monochrome, separated by hairlines.

Settings: count (4 | 6) · tone (ink | muted)

Mobile: Wraps to two rows; never scrolls by itself.

### `single-large-quote`

One quote at large size given a whole band.

Settings: scale (large | display) · portrait (none | supplied)

Mobile: Steps down one rung.

### `figure-strip`

Three or four real figures in a row with units and sources.

Settings: count (3 | 4) · divider (hairline | space)

Mobile: Two by two, then one column.
