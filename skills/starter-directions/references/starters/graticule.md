# Graticule

A sheet ruled by its own grid: twelve columns shown as hairlines, one heavy grotesque, one signal
ink on geometry.

## Suits

Design studios, systems, architecture, typographic brands.

## Avoid

Warm or narrative brands; content that does not fit a grid.

## Axes

axes: ground=light display=sans accent=yellow

## Surface and colour

Near-white sheet; ink black-ish; the grid drawn as hairlines in a light neutral; one signal yellow
used on a geometric shape, never on text.

## Type roles

One heavy grotesque at several sizes for everything; small figures for indices. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `index-first`, `work-grid`, `tile-field`. Parts: `margin-label`, `edge-aligned`,
`dense-type`.

## Object family

Grid lines, circles, squares, index numbers.

## Signature moves

1. The column grid visible as hairlines at rest.
2. Everything aligns to it except one element that breaks it on purpose.
3. The signal ink on one geometric shape per screen.

## Motion

None, or a single shape that moves along the grid on scroll; reduced motion removes it.

## Ambition

Floor: strict alignment. Target: the grid is the identity. Ceiling: content that reflows visibly
along the grid.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band; signal yellow never carries text (contrast).
