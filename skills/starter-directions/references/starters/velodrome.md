# Velodrome

Fast and numerate: condensed roman type, results in tabular figures, and one horizontal sweep.

## Suits

Sport, events, timing, results, fitness brands.

## Avoid

Slow or contemplative content.

## Axes

axes: ground=mid display=condensed accent=teal

## Surface and colour

Concrete mid-grey ground; ink near-black; a teal accent for live or leading values.

## Type roles

A condensed roman sans for display in uppercase (leading ≥ 1.0); a plain sans for text; tabular
figures for every result. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `evidence-led`, `catalogue`, `photograph-led`. Parts: `evidence-figure`, `figure-strip`,
`three-zone-bar`, `category-index`.

## Object family

Results tables, lap times, lane lines, supplied action photography.

## Signature moves

1. Results set in tabular figures, leaders in the accent.
2. One horizontal sweep that reveals a results row on entry.
3. Lane-line rules between sections.

## Motion

One horizontal sweep per page; everything else static. Reduced motion shows the row in place.

## Ambition

Floor: results readable at a glance. Target: it feels fast. Ceiling: live results that update in
place.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band. Never show a result, time or ranking the brief did not
supply.
