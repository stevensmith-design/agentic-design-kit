# Inkspill

Two-colour print on coloured paper: overprinted shapes, a little grain, and heavy lowercase type.

## Suits

Independent shops, events, zines, makers, culture listings.

## Avoid

Finance, health, anything that must read as sober.

## Axes

axes: ground=light display=sans accent=pink

## Surface and colour

Pale warm pink paper; ink a dark blue-black; a second ink (fluorescent pink) overprinted with a
multiply blend so overlaps darken. A fine grain overlay at very low opacity.

## Type roles

A heavy grotesque in lowercase for display; a sturdy sans for text; no outlier, or a mono for dates.
No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `tile-field`, `work-grid`, `catalogue`. Parts: `split-pair`, `item-cards`,
`single-line`.

## Object family

Overprinted geometric shapes, halftone dots drawn in CSS, misregistered outlines.

## Signature moves

1. Shapes and headings offset by a few pixels in the second ink, as if printed out of register.
2. Overprint: the second ink multiplies where it crosses the first.
3. One hand-cut shape per section, never the same twice.

## Motion

Static. A hover nudges the misregistration by a pixel or two.

## Ambition

Floor: two inks, used with discipline. Target: it looks printed. Ceiling: one poster-like section.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band — the second ink as a surface counts as a ground, not the
accent. Contrast is checked on the pink paper, not on white.
