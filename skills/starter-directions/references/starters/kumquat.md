# Kumquat

Warm, round and calm: a mid-grey paper, one citrus accent, soft controls.

## Suits

Consumer apps, services, friendly B2B, onboarding pages.

## Avoid

Editorial or technical content; brands that need edge.

## Axes

axes: ground=mid display=rounded accent=orange

## Surface and colour

Warm mid-grey paper; ink a warm charcoal; raised surfaces one step lighter. A citrus orange accent
on primary actions only.

## Type roles

A rounded or soft geometric sans for display and text; no outlier. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `feature-stack`, `walkthrough`, `question-led`. Parts: `split-pair`,
`annotated-capture`, `outlined-chip`, `subscribe-first`.

## Object family

Real product screens, unframed; soft geometric shapes drawn in CSS.

## Signature moves

1. Pill-shaped primary actions in the accent; everything else outlined.
2. Real screens set on a lighter raised surface, no device frame.
3. Section heads in sentence case with generous space above.

## Motion

Quiet: a short ease on hover and focus; state changes slide by a few pixels.

## Ambition

Floor: friendly and clear. Target: calm confidence. Ceiling: one screen that animates a real flow.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band. Contrast checked on the mid-grey paper, which is easy to
under-shoot.
