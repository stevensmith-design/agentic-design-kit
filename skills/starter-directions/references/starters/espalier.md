# Espalier

A kitchen garden on paper: cream ground, an ornamental display used sparingly, leaf green, and drawn
botanical rules.

## Suits

Food, gardens, crafts, hospitality, local makers.

## Avoid

Technology products, dense data.

## Axes

axes: ground=light display=decorative accent=green

## Surface and colour

Cream paper; ink a deep brown-green; a leaf green accent; one soft secondary neutral for raised
surfaces.

## Type roles

An ornamental display face used only for the largest heading per screen; a warm text serif for
everything else. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `photograph-led`, `letter`, `walkthrough`. Parts: `full-bleed-photo`, `hanging-head`,
`letter-close`, `minimal-pair`.

## Object family

Hand-drawn SVG botanical rules and corner marks; supplied photography of the real produce or place.

## Signature moves

1. Section dividers drawn as a growing stem, each a little different.
2. The ornamental face once per screen, never twice.
3. Captions under every photograph, saying where and when.

## Motion

None at rest; a drawn rule may grow once when it enters, and not under reduced motion.

## Ambition

Floor: warm and orderly. Target: it smells of the place. Ceiling: a seasonal page that changes with
the calendar.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band. The ornamental face never sets text below display size.
