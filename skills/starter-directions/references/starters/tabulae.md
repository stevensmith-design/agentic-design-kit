# Tabulae

A ledger: dense, aligned, numerate. Hierarchy comes from columns and tabular figures, not from size.

## Suits

Data products, reference sites, archives, pricing and specification-heavy pages.

## Avoid

Emotional or story-led brands; very little content.

## Axes

axes: ground=light display=sans accent=blue

## Surface and colour

Cool light paper; ink near-black with a blue cast; zebra-free tables with hairline rows. The accent
is an ink blue for links and the one figure that matters.

## Type roles

A plain grotesque for display at modest size; the same family for text; mono or tabular figures as
the outlier for numbers and labels. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `index-first`, `catalogue`, `evidence-led`. Parts: `spec-table`, `figure-strip`,
`three-zone-bar`, `dense-type`.

## Object family

Tables, small multiples, tick marks, labelled axes.

## Signature moves

1. Every number in tabular figures, right-aligned in its column.
2. Column headers in small mono labels.
3. One sparkline or small multiple drawn from real data in the first screen.

## Motion

Sorting and filtering animate row order with a short slide; nothing decorative moves.

## Ambition

Floor: everything aligns. Target: the page reads like a well-kept ledger. Ceiling: an interactive
table that is the hero.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band. Density follows `rules.md` → *Data tables*; tap targets
stay 44px on touch.
