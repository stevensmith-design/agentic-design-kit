# Placard

A poster pasted to a wall: one flooded band of colour, very heavy type, and short blunt sentences.

## Suits

Campaigns, launches, causes, events with one message.

## Avoid

Anything with many features or a long argument.

## Axes

axes: ground=light display=sans accent=red

## Surface and colour

Light paper with one band flooded in red as a surface (a ground token, not the accent); ink black.
The accent stays small: link states and one mark.

## Type roles

A very heavy sans in uppercase for display, leading at 1.0 or more; a plain sans for text. No
outlier. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `headline-first`, `declaration`. Parts: `headline-only`, `slab-bar`,
`closing-statement`.

## Object family

Type, flat colour, arrows drawn as heavy strokes.

## Signature moves

1. One section flooded edge to edge in the flood colour, type knocked out of it.
2. Headlines that fill the measure at their size.
3. A closing line that repeats the headline's verb.

## Motion

None, or one hard cut between bands on scroll. Nothing eases in.

## Ambition

Floor: one message, said loudly and legibly. Target: it stops a scroll. Ceiling: the flood takes the
whole first screen.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band; the flood is a surface and is contrast-checked as one.
