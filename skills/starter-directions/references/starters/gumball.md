# Gumball

Cream paper, several bright inks, rounded type and one drawn character with a moment of its own.

## Suits

Games, children's products (with care), playful consumer apps, community events.

## Avoid

Serious services, finance, health.

## Axes

axes: ground=light display=rounded accent=pink

## Surface and colour

Cream paper; ink a warm dark; a full palette of three or four named inks (pink leads), each with one
job. Never a gradient between them.

## Type roles

A heavy rounded sans for display; the same family lighter for text. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `tile-field`, `feature-stack`, `question-led`. Parts: `drawn-centrepiece`,
`tile-cluster`, `inline-form`, `running-ticker`.

## Object family

One hand-drawn SVG character made for the brand; stickers and badges drawn the same way.

## Signature moves

1. One character moment: the character reacts once, to one real event.
2. Each ink owns one job across the page.
3. Tiles in different inks, never two neighbours alike.

## Motion

Motion is part of the voice: short, springy only on the character, never on state changes; reduced
motion freezes the character in its best pose.

## Ambition

Floor: cheerful and legible. Target: it makes people smile once. Ceiling: the character guides the
first use.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band per ink; the full palette is `rules.md` *Full palette*,
documented once.
