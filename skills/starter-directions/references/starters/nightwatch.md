# Nightwatch

A dark page that makes a stand: heavy condensed capitals, a few numbered statements, one red bleed.

## Suits

Manifestos, studios with a stance, launches that argue.

## Avoid

Products that need demonstrating; gentle or caring brands.

## Axes

axes: ground=dark display=condensed accent=red

## Surface and colour

Near-black ground tinted warm; ink off-white; one band where a deep red bleeds from the edge as a
surface. The accent is reserved for one key word per statement.

## Type roles

A condensed heavy sans in uppercase for display (leading ≥ 1.0); a neutral sans for text. No italic
display.

## Composition

Structures: `declaration`, `headline-first`, `long-read`. Parts: `margin-label`, `edge-aligned`,
`closing-statement`.

## Object family

Numerals, rules, a single cropped photograph if supplied.

## Signature moves

1. One key word per statement set in the accent, at display size.
2. A red bleed entering from one edge of one section.
3. Copy in short declarative lines, no hedging.

## Motion

Statements are present at load. One slow fade between sections at most; reduced motion removes it.

## Ambition

Floor: dark done legibly. Target: the stance is unmissable. Ceiling: the bleed covers the first
screen.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band; the bleed is a surface. Body text never below AA on the
dark ground; no pure black or pure white.
