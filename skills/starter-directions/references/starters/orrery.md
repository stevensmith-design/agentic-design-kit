# Orrery

One precise instrument drawn alone in a dark field, labelled like a technical plate.

## Suits

Measurement, evaluation, science, precision tools — a product with one mechanism worth drawing.

## Avoid

Products with no single mechanism; playful or crowded brands.

## Axes

axes: ground=dark display=serif accent=yellow

## Surface and colour

Near-black ground with a cool cast; ink off-white; a brass-yellow accent reserved for the
instrument's working parts. Only the instrument carries light.

## Type roles

A lowercase classical serif for display; small uppercase mono for labels and callouts; a neutral
sans for text. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `diagram-led`, `evidence-led`. Parts: `drawn-centrepiece`, `figure-strip`,
`edge-aligned`, `closing-statement`.

## Object family

One hand-built SVG apparatus — a gauge, a mechanism, an orbit — with leader-line callouts.

## Signature moves

1. The instrument alone in the first screen, with generous dead space around it.
2. Leader-line callouts in small mono capitals.
3. One accent-coloured word in the headline.

## Motion

Static, or one slow movement of the instrument; reduced motion holds it still.

## Ambition

Floor: a correct drawing. Target: the instrument explains the product. Ceiling: the instrument
responds to real input.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band. The instrument is labelled (`role=img` + title) or hidden
from assistive tech with the callouts as text.
