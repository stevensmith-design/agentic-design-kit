# Quire

A page that reads like a well-set book: quiet paper, a confident serif, and numbers in the margin
doing the navigation.

## Suits

Long writing, essays, studios and practices that are explained rather than sold; audiences who read.

## Avoid

Dashboards, dense product UI, anything the visitor scans in seconds.

## Axes

axes: ground=light display=serif accent=red

## Surface and colour

Warm off-white paper tinted toward the accent; ink a warm near-black; rules as hairlines in a mid
neutral. The accent is a deep red used for the margin facts, one link state and nothing else.

## Type roles

A high-contrast serif for display, set large and tight; a text serif or a humanist sans for reading
at 60–70ch; small figures for the margin facts. No italic display.

## Composition

Structures: `long-read`, `letter`, `index-first`. Parts: `margin-label`, `text-link-cta`,
`colophon-mast`, `minimal-pair`.

## Object family

Rules, figures, footnote marks, a colophon. No icons, no illustration.

## Signature moves

1. Each section's key fact in its own margin column, aligned to the first line of the section — a
   fact, not a sequence number.
2. A full-measure hairline under each section head.
3. A closing colophon that says how the page was made.

## Motion

None at rest. Links underline on hover with an offset; nothing reveals on scroll.

## Ambition

Floor: a calm, correct reading page. Target: it feels set, not styled. Ceiling: one figure breaking
out to full bleed.

## Limits

Accent at most 5 % of any viewport band. Display steps follow `rules.md` headline brackets; never
past the 1280×800 fold-fit test.
