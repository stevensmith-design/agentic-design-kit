Three commitments

The system is derived

Your brand guideline becomes a token graph with the relationships solved, not guessed — contrast, value steps, a neutral ramp carrying your hue.

The system is written down

A DESIGN.md that states what the system commits to and why, so a reviewer can disagree with a decision instead of a vibe.

The system is enforced

Every screen is checked against the graph before it ships. A failed relationship is a defect with a line number, not a note in a comment thread.

Evidence a review produces

Not a slide of opinions. A list you can close.

  • Fail Muted text on card falls to 3.9:1 — below AA.
  • Review Two radii within 1px of each other read as one step.
  • Pass Neutral ramp derived from the brand hue at 0.8% chroma.
