# Direction contract — one per direction, provisional until selected

Write this after the direction has been proposed. Freeze it before building the proof; after selection,
change it only through an amendment.

```markdown
# Direction — <name>
Status: provisional | selected <date> | reserved | killed
Route: reference-led | reference-directed | brief-originated

## Premise
What the experience is trying to make felt and how the real content behaves.

## Reference responsibilities
| Original source | Quality used | Content or functional connection | Must not transfer |
|---|---|---|---|

Closeness and human-approved traits: link dated discovery answers; do not invent approval.
Lead reference and linked provisional visual guide; contribution to overall source coverage.
Reference use: faithful style | loose inspiration | selected combination (and why compatible).
Preserve: observed colour roles/coverage, type character, component and imagery relationships.
Adapt: how this content changes composition, hierarchy and reading behaviour.
Departures: project-driven or new choices, their real basis, and any material conflict/decision.
Do not attribute a constraint-driven value or proposed substitute to the source.

## Key choices
Composition · hierarchy · type behaviour · imagery/material · colour behaviour · interaction/motion ·
responsive intent. Record only choices material to this direction.

## Exclusions
The incompatible or identity-bound moves this direction deliberately avoids.

## Reference-directed limits, when applicable
The person's request in their words; what may be followed closely; what remains non-transferable.

## Proof scope
The smallest render or sequence needed to judge the decisive choices; relevant mobile and reduced-motion
views; production pages and systems explicitly out of scope.

## Real-asset constraints
Verified assets, missing evidence and anything that must not be invented.
Direction-specific image plan: role, source/generation brief, placement, fallback and label.
Link permission for generated placeholders, proposed mockups, shared assets or type-only treatment.
Study sample content is labelled and internally consistent; claims are never invented as facts.

## Provenance
Link the post-proposal decision-provenance record when one is useful.
```
