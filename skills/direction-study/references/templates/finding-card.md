# Finding card — optional observations beside an original reference

Step 3. Use when written observations will help. The card stays linked to the original; synthesis and
builders receive the original itself as well.

```markdown
# Source S<n> — finding card
Original: <path or URL>
Inspected as: still | sequence of stills | motion | URL render | local file | NOT INSPECTED

## Visual inspection evidence
- Original artifact viewed: <path or URL>
- Rendered as: <pixel dimensions | frame range and duration | URL viewport and date>
- Visible facts: <three to five concrete observations proving the artifact itself was viewed>

## Findings
| ID | Axis | Transferable observation | Why it matters visually |
|---|---|---|
| S<n>-F1 | | |

## Prohibited-transfer traits
| ID | Trait (abstract) | Why it must not transfer |
|---|---|---|
| S<n>-X1 | | identity-bound · signature device · proprietary · copy |

## Unknowns
What could not be determined from what was inspected.
```

Rules: preserve enough visible specificity to communicate the person's taste. For style adaptation,
use `reference-visual-guide.md` as well; a palette alone is insufficient. Do not transfer a source's
logo, branded copy, proprietary artwork or identity mark. Adapt composition to the real content;
explicitly authorized recreation follows its recorded limits. Colour values may be recorded with
their roles and evidence. Verify font identity where possible, distinguish proposed substitutes,
and check usage rights for font files or other assets before reusing them.

**NOT INSPECTED** — the source could not be retrieved or opened. The card then has no findings and
no prohibited traits and says why in Unknowns. If it is essential to the request, ask for an accessible
copy; otherwise name the limitation. Never fill it from memory.
