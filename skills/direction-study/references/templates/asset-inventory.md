# Asset inventory — evidence honesty

Step 1, whenever a direction will show or play project material: screenshots, product imagery,
photography, clips, audio, or structured data (a catalogue, prices, figures). It keeps
project evidence apart from inspiration, so nothing is invented to fill a gap.

## Missing — and stays missing

| Project / item | What is missing | How a direction shows the gap |
|---|---|---|
| | | text only · honest placeholder · ask the person |

## Inspiration — not listed here

References and clips remain directly available as art-direction evidence and are **never promoted to
project evidence** or shown as if they were the product. An assigned reference may enter its concept
builder; it never becomes page content. Keep inspiration and product evidence clearly labelled.

## No-invention rules

- No generated or redrawn product interfaces, fake devices, mock dashboards or stock substitutes.
- Do not tint, recolour or crop a real asset to suit a direction; solve the contrast honestly.
- An unavailable source is recorded as unavailable — not replaced with something similar.
- A comparison control (an existing baseline) is for comparison only; no fragments are reused.
