# Direction premise — written from the brief and original references

```markdown
# Direction <letter> — <working name>

## Intended experience
What should the audience feel, understand or do?

## Content behaviour
How do the real claims, evidence, sequence and assets behave in this direction?

## Reference responsibilities
| Original source | Useful quality | How this project transforms it | What must not transfer |
|---|---|---|---|

## Decisive choices
The few composition, type, material, image, colour, motion or responsive choices that make this
direction worth testing.

## Test surface
The smallest proof that could validate or disprove the direction.
```

There is no required naming scheme, governing mechanism, stock-archetype prediction or default number
of premises. Use the brief and supplied evidence.
