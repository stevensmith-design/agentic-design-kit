# Brief partition — invariants, variables, test surfaces

Step 2 of a study. Written once, before any direction exists, and shared by every direction. A
brief that silently fixes surface, type, palette, density and structure leaves nothing to explore —
this template makes that visible before it wastes a round.

## Reference intent and assets — human answers before directions

Closeness: loose inspiration | rearranged | close (record permitted close-work limits).
Observed traits: 3–5 concrete visible traits per original, with the originals directly accessible.
Reference/rule conflicts: trait → approved | declined | qualified, dated answer and actual locator;
if no gated traits, record that explicitly. Approval applies to proofs and preserves quality floors.
Imagery permission: supplied | generated placeholders | labelled proposed mockups | type-only agreed.
Image dependency and direction-specific plans: role, source, placement, fallback, labels.
Missing content/name/language answers only where material. Do not invent verified claims.
