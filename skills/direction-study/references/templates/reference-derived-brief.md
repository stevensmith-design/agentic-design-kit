# Reference-informed creative brief — optional synthesis aid

Use this when several sources need a shared reading. It is written while the originals are directly
available and remains beside them; it does not replace them or precede all creative thought as a gate.

```markdown
# Reference-informed creative brief — <project>

## Project intent and real content
Audience, purpose, evidence, sequence and asset constraints.

## Per-source visual guides and requested influence
Link each original and its provisional visual guide. Record whether the sources are alternative
styles or deliberate contributions to a combination, and the person's influence preference.

## Useful reference relationships
Concrete observations about colour roles/coverage, hierarchy, type, imagery, components, material,
density, interaction or motion. Keep observations separate from proposed adaptations and unknowns.

## Productive tensions
Where the sources suggest genuinely different possibilities.

## Non-transferable qualities
Identity marks, proprietary assets, copy and recognisable treatments that remain with their sources.

## Possible directions
Positive premises suggested by the brief, content and source relationships. Use as many as the request
and material justify; do not force a stock set or equal source distribution.
```
