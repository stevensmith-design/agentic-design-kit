# Divergence check — are these directions materially different?

Run after directions have been proposed and before investing in proofs. This is a comparison aid, not
an aesthetic specification.

Compare the dimensions that matter to this brief, which may include:

- content hierarchy and sequence;
- composition and spatial rhythm;
- typographic behaviour;
- image, object or evidence treatment;
- material and colour behaviour;
- interaction, motion and responsive intent;
- treatment of the hardest real asset or state.

Judge meaningful differences against what the person is deciding. When contrasting references are
alternative styles, different type systems, colour balance, component language and image treatments
can offer a substantive choice on the same content structure. Do not invent unrelated page layouts
or signature devices to force divergence. When structural alternatives are requested, a palette or
copy swap does not satisfy them. Revise or remove options that do not expose a useful decision.
Do not force every direction into the same table, proof shape, page skeleton or named mechanism,
and do not make one deliberately weak to create contrast.
