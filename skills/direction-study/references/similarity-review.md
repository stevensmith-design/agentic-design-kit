# Reference resonance and similarity review

Run read-only after rendering, preferably in a fresh context. Use two input stages so the builder's
interpretation cannot define what counts as a match.

1. Give the reviewer only the original brief, actual reference images and the person's discovery
   answers. Before showing contracts, exclusions or builder notes, save 3–5 defining visible traits
   per reference in an originals-first observation report. Record unavailable isolation honestly.
2. Then reveal the renders, reference assignments and provisional contracts. Compare against the
   saved observations and human intent. A builder's exclusion is not human approval to discard a
   reference trait. Report both lost influence and excessive resemblance.

For each result, ask:

- Are the requested qualities visibly present, or did rule compliance erase their character?
- Were those qualities transformed by this project's content and function?
- Against the lead reference, identify at least **two visible structural changes**: for example,
  hero composition, section sequence, content hierarchy or the signature object. Colour, font or
  copy swaps alone do not count. Two changes do not automatically establish enough distance.
- Is a composition, motion, object, artwork, copy or identity element recognisably copied?
- Does the result assemble recognisable fragments from several sources?
- In explicitly authorized close recreation, judge the recorded permission and limits instead of
  demanding two changes that contradict the request. Cite that permission, never infer it.

Report **distinct and resonant**, **under-influenced**, **derivative on <axis>**, or **too close**
with visible evidence. Name the lead source and structural changes, or the authorized close-work
exception. This review cannot clear genericness or mechanical quality. Do not repair the proof or
introduce a stock-archetype or house-style test absent from the brief.
