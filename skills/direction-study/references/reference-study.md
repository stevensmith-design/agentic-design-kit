# Reference study — preserve useful influence without copying

Open the actual originals and keep them visible during synthesis and proof-building. For each,
record **3–5 concrete observed defining traits**: what is visibly happening in composition, type,
colour, imagery, depth or interaction. Write observations before deciding exclusions. Abstract
phrases such as “professional” or “colour has a job” do not preserve the source's visual character.
Then connect useful traits to this project's content and say what identity-bound material must not
transfer. Do not treat a common visual technique as proprietary or automatically defective.

## Settle intent before proposing directions

Ask once, together, unless the person already answered:

- **Closeness:** loose inspiration, rearranged, or close? Loose and rearranged both use the
  reference-led route, with different influence strength. Close uses reference-directed only with
  explicit permission and recorded limits. Keep originals visible in every mode.
- **Reference/rule conflicts:** show the observed traits that meet Tier-2 gates and ask which to
  approve, decline or qualify. Do not replace them with flat/default treatments while waiting and
  call that a completed study. Record the dated human answer and its actual transcript locator.
  If none are gated, say so briefly. This is study authority for those traits, not production
  system approval or a waiver of accessibility/craft requirements.
- **Image dependency:** does the character depend on photography, illustration, cutouts, rendered
  objects or product UI? Ask whether supplied assets, labelled generated placeholders or proposed
  study mockups are permitted, or whether the person accepts a type-only reinterpretation.
  Resolve a working name, content outline or language only where missing information materially
  changes the proof; reuse known answers instead of repeating discovery.

Plan imagery separately for each direction before building: role, source or generation brief,
placement, fallback and labels. Do not silently make all proofs type-only or share one asset pool.
If tools/quota limit imagery, disclose the constraint and agree on shared assets or a reduced scope.
Study mockups may demonstrate a proposed experience, but cannot claim an existing feature, customer
result or verified capability. Distinguish illustrative sample content from facts.

## Coverage without forced blending

Each supplied reference must lead or contribute to at least one direction. State the concrete
contribution; do not count a token colour mention as meaningful coverage. One source may lead one
proof while others contribute elsewhere. Never average all sources into every direction. If a
source is unsuitable, explain why and obtain confirmation before omitting it.

A reference responsibility or exclusion written by the builder is provisional. It cannot silently
cancel the person's stated preferences or make lost reference character acceptable to a reviewer.
Stabilize temporary files when possible; disclose any inaccessible source. Notes and custody
receipts help memory and access, but never substitute for viewing the images.
